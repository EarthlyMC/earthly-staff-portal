SUPPORT SYSTEM

Ticket Guide

Check the category before claiming or responding to a ticket.

🎫 ALL STAFF

General Tickets

General questions, help requests and issues that don't fit another category.

Handled by: Any staff member
🐛 DEV / TM

Bug Reports

Players reporting bugs, glitches, broken features or technical problems.

Handled by: Developers or Ticket Management
👤 ALL STAFF

Player Reports

Reports involving player behaviour, rule breaking or other player-related issues.

Handled by: Any staff member
🚨 TICKET MANAGEMENT

Staff Reports

Reports or complaints involving a member of the Earthly staff team.

Handled by: Ticket Management only
🗺️ MAPPERS

Nation Applications

Applications or requests relating to nations and mapping.

Handled by: Mappers
💳 DEV / TM

Purchase Support

Issues with purchases, store products, ranks, items or payment-related support.

Handled by: Developers or Ticket Management
⚖️ TICKET MANAGEMENT

Punishment Appeals

Players appealing a punishment received on the server.

Handled by: Ticket Management
❓ DEPENDENT

Other Issues

Issues that don't fit an existing category.

Handled by: Depends on the issue

TEAM STRUCTURE

Staff Roles

Understand when to handle, escalate or hand over an issue.

👑

Owner

Oversees Earthly as a whole, makes final decisions and manages the senior staff team.

🧑‍💼

Management

Handles staff management, escalated situations, major decisions and overall server operations.

💻

Developers

Handle technical issues, bug reports, purchase support and development-related problems.

🎫

Ticket Management

Manages the ticket system, handles restricted categories and escalates issues where necessary.

🗺️

Mappers

Review and manage Nation Applications and mapping-related requests.

🛡️

Moderation

Helps enforce server rules, handle player issues and maintain a fair environment.

💬

Helpers

Assist players with questions and basic support. Escalate anything outside your permissions.

🔼
Escalation rule

If something is outside your role, don't guess. Pass it to the correct team or ask a senior staff member.

STAFF STANDARD

Staff Guidelines

The standards expected from everyone representing Earthly.

01

Be professional

Speak respectfully to players and staff. You represent Earthly whenever you act as staff.

02

Don't abuse permissions

Staff permissions exist to help the server. Never use them for personal gain, jokes or retaliation.

03

Stay active

Keep up with staff discussions, tickets and important announcements relevant to your role.

04

Keep information private

Don't leak private staff discussions, reports, evidence or internal information.

05

Ask when unsure

It is always better to ask another staff member than make a decision you aren't confident about.

06

Don't interfere

Don't take over tickets or situations assigned to another team unless asked or required.

07

Be fair

Treat players equally. Personal friendships or disagreements must not affect staff decisions.

08

Follow the chain of command

Escalate issues through the appropriate staff structure rather than creating unnecessary conflict.
